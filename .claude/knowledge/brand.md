# Brand Knowledge — INGLY DESIGN

## Brand Essence
INGLY DESIGN è uno studio artigianale digitale che unisce tecnologia laser di precisione con la creatività del design personalizzato. Il brand posiziona la tecnologia come strumento di espressione, non come fine.

**Tagline:** "Creatività senza limiti. Precisione senza compromessi."
**Tone of voice:** Caldo, preciso, artigianale, orgoglioso. Mai corporate, mai generico.

### Typography
- **Headings:** `var(--fd)` — Cormorant Garamond (serif elegante, artigianale)
- **Body:** `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif` (fast, no layout shift)
- **Type scale:** 1.25 ratio — `--fs-2xs` through `--fs-4xl`
- **Weight:** 400 regular, 600 semibold for UI, 700 bold for headings

### Logo
- File: `assets/images/logo.png`
- Usage: navigation bar (40px height), favicon set, og-image
- Clear space: minimum 16px on all sides
- Dark/light: same logo works on both (light-colored logo)

### Brand Voice

#### Do
- "Inciso al laser CO₂ su betulla da 3mm" (specific, technical, honest)
- "Personalizzabile con nome, data o messaggio" (concrete benefit)
- "Consegna in 3 giorni lavorativi" (reliable promise)
- "Made in Cesena, spedito in tutta Italia" (local pride)

#### Don't
- "Prodotto di altissima qualità" (generic, meaningless)
- "Perfetto per ogni occasione" (lazy, no specificity)
- "Straordinario, meraviglioso, fantastico" (hyperbole)
- "Il miglior negozio online" (unprovable)

## Business Context

### Production capabilities
| Technology | Primary materials | Throughput |
|-----------|------------------|-----------|
| CO₂ Laser | Wood, acrylic, leather, paper | High volume |
| MOPA Fiber | Stainless steel, aluminum, anodized | Medium |
| UV Flatbed | Any rigid surface up to 60×90cm | Medium |
| DTF | All fabrics, 100% cotton best | High volume |
| FDM 3D | PLA, PETG, TPU, ABS | Low/medium |

### Competitive advantages
1. **No minimum quantity** — even 1 piece accepted
2. **3-day turnaround** (standard), 1-2 days (express)
3. **Multi-technology** — one supplier for laser, UV, DTF, 3D
4. **Custom artwork included** — free design work for all orders
5. **B2B pricing** — bulk discounts from 10 pieces

### Target customers
1. **Private customers (B2C)**
   - Gift buyers (birthday, anniversary, birth, wedding)
   - Home decor enthusiasts
   - Pet owners (medagliette)
   - Event organizers (weddings, christenings, parties)

2. **Business customers (B2B)**
   - Local businesses (branded merchandise, awards)
   - Event agencies (personalized gifts, place cards)
   - Interior designers (custom decor pieces)
   - Craftspeople needing CNC/laser work (conto terzi)

3. **Digital creators**
   - Makers buying SVG/DXF/STL files
   - Laser cutting enthusiasts buying tested templates

### Geographic focus
- Primary: Cesena, Forlì-Cesena, Emilia-Romagna
- Secondary: Italy-wide (free shipping over €80)
- Future: EU expansion (currently shipping to IT only)
