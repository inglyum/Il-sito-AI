## Production Technologies

### CO2 Laser (main workhorse)
- **Materials:** Wood (birch, walnut, oak, MDF, bamboo), Acrylic/Plexiglass, Leather, Fabric, Paper, Cardboard
- **NOT suitable for:** Metal (direct), Polycarbonate, PVC, ABS
- **Key strengths:** Clean cuts on wood and acrylic, excellent raster engraving, fast for large areas
- **Speed parameters:** Cutting plywood 3mm: 15-25mm/s / Engraving at 300dpi: 400-600mm/s
- **Resolution:** 300-600 DPI for photos, 1000 DPI for fine text
- **Use cases:** Decorative items, signage, packaging, awards, gifts

### MOPA Fiber Laser (premium precision)
- **Materials:** Stainless steel, aluminum, titanium, brass, copper, anodized aluminum, some plastics
- **Key strengths:** Color engraving on stainless (360 colors via oxidation), deep engraving, high precision
- **Special capability:** Color marks WITHOUT paint — purely physical/chemical process, permanent
- **Use cases:** Industrial parts, pet tags, jewelry, high-end awards, medical devices
- **Taglines:** "Incisione permanente che non si cancella mai" / "Colori senza inchiostro"

### UV Printing (flatbed)
- **Materials:** ANY rigid flat surface: wood, metal, glass, ceramic, leather, plastic, stone, phone cases
- **Key strengths:** Full-color CMYK+W, photo-realistic prints, raised tactile effects (3D varnish)
- **Maximum dimensions:** typically up to 60x90cm per pass
- **Use cases:** Personalized gifts, awards, phone cases, tiles, photo on wood, branded merchandise
- **Advantage over vinyl:** No peeling, no bubbles, washable, permanent

### DTF (Direct to Film)
- **Materials:** 100% cotton, cotton blends, polyester, dark fabrics (white underbase)
- **Key strengths:** No minimum quantity, no setup fees, complex multicolor designs, photo quality on fabric
- **Process:** Print → hot peel film → heat press transfer → washed immediately
- **Use cases:** T-shirts, hoodies, tote bags, uniforms, custom merchandise

### 3D Printing (FDM)
- **Materials:** PLA (standard), PETG (flexible/food-safe), TPU (rubber-like), ABS (heat resistant)
- **Layer heights:** 0.1mm (fine), 0.2mm (standard), 0.3mm (fast/draft)
- **Use cases:** Prototypes, custom parts, cosplay props, organizational items, display stands, keychains
- **Lead time:** 1-3 days depending on size and complexity
